Method

How this is scored

Each pick is compared to the median points scored by every other pick in the same round of the same draft. Beat that median and the pick is worth positive value; fall short and it isn't.

Scoring a player means the fantasy points he put up that season, in this league's own scoring settings — not a career total and not somebody else's rankings.

Keepers are excluded. They weren't really drafted, and counting them would flatter whoever held onto a star.

Like everything else here, it's arithmetic over Sleeper's own data. No opinions, no model, same answer every time.
